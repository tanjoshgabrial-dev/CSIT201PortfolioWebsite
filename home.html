Welcome to Jollibee!

Jollibee is the Philippines' most beloved fast food chain, known for its delicious fried chicken, spaghetti, and many other mouth-watering meals loved by Filipinos of all ages.

From humble beginnings as an ice cream parlor in 1975, Jollibee has grown into a global brand with thousands of branches around the world.


Why People Love Jollibee

  • Famous Chickenjoy - crispy and juicy fried chicken
  • Jolly Spaghetti - sweet-style spaghetti Filipinos love
  • Affordable and delicious meals
  • A taste of home for Filipinos worldwide
