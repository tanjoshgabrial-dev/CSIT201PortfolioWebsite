About Jollibee

Jollibee was founded by Tony Tan Caktiong in 1975 in Quezon City, Philippines. It started as a small ice cream parlor before expanding into a full fast food restaurant serving Filipino-style meals.

Today, Jollibee is one of the largest fast food chains in Asia, with thousands of branches not just in the Philippines but also in countries like the United States, Canada, Hong Kong, and more.


Our Mission

Jollibee aims to serve great-tasting food that brings joy to every Filipino family. We are committed to delivering high-quality meals at affordable prices in a fun and welcoming environment.
